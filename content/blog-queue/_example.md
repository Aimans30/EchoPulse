## What changes the price of a listing video?

Length and revisions change the price most. A **listing walkthrough** is a 60 to 90 second video that moves through the home room by room. Vendor C's pricing page adds $25 for each extra minute.

- Music licensing is included by Vendor D and Vendor F.
- Captions cost extra at Vendor B.

## Worked example: an agent with 4 listings a month

An agent with 4 listings a month at Vendor C's $90 a video spends $360 a month. At Vendor A's $40 the same month costs $160.

## Which option should an agent pick?

Pick a per-video service under $100, such as Vendor A, Vendor B or Vendor C in the table above, if you list fewer than 3 homes a month. Above that, a monthly plan costs less per video. The opposite is right when every listing needs drone footage.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to edit a real estate video?

Editing one listing video costs $40 to $250 across the 6 example vendors in the table above. The price depends on length and on how many rounds of changes are included.

### How long does listing video editing take?

The vendors in the table state 2 to 5 days. Vendor E does not publish a turnaround.

### Is a monthly plan cheaper than paying per video?

It is once you list 3 or more homes a month. Below that, paying per video costs less.

### Do editors need the raw footage or a finished cut?

Editors work from raw footage. Send every clip, including the ones you think are unusable.
