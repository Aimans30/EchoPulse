## How does each AI tool pick its sources?

### ChatGPT

[OpenAI's crawler page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) says sites "opted out of OAI-SearchBot will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers." GPTBot is the separate training crawler, so blocking it does not remove you from search.

OpenAI's [help page on ChatGPT search](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) says ChatGPT "sometimes partners with other search providers", and links Microsoft's and Shopify's privacy policies as providers' policies. Placement "is not guaranteed."

### Perplexity

[Perplexity's crawler docs](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers) say PerplexityBot surfaces and links sites in answers and is not used for model training. Allow it in robots.txt and, by its published IP ranges, in your firewall.

### Google AI Overviews and AI Mode

Google's [AI features page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) sets two conditions: the page is indexed and eligible for a snippet. The [AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) adds a third: the site must be included in the Search generative AI control in Search Console. Include is the [default for every property](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024?hl=en).

Google's [robots meta tag page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots-meta-tag) says nosnippet stops content being used as direct input for AI Overviews and AI Mode, and max-snippet limits how much is used. [Google-Extended](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/google-extended) does not control either feature. It covers model training and grounding in Gemini apps, outside Search.

### Copilot, Claude and Gemini

Copilot Chat in Microsoft 365 sends web queries "to Bing", per [Microsoft's privacy page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/privacy-and-protections). Bing supports [IndexNow](https://www.indexnow.org/), a ping that tells search engines a URL changed. Google is not on IndexNow's list of supporters.

Anthropic's [crawler page](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler) says blocking Claude-SearchBot "may reduce your site's visibility" in Claude's search results.

For Gemini apps, Google's [30 October 2026 post](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/10/ai-training-control) says sites that allow Google-Extended "are eligible to appear in responses and receive clicks via grounding."

## What do the studies say, and where do they disagree?

They agree that ranking well helps, and disagree widely on how much. These are correlations from vendor or panel data; none proves that one change causes a citation.

| Question | Study A | Study B | Why they differ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Share of AI Overview citations in Google's top 10 | Ahrefs, July 2025: 76.1% | Ahrefs, March 2026: 37.9% | Top 3 citations vs all; AI Overviews moved to Gemini 3 in January 2026 |

### Rankings and mentions

[Ahrefs' July 2025 study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/search-rankings-ai-citations/) counted 1.9 million citations; its [March 2026 update](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10/) counted 4 million URLs with a changed method, so not all of the drop is Google. [Conductor](https://www.conductor.com/academy/aio-vs-organic-rankings/), across nearly 168 million citations, found position one cited 24.9% of the time and position ten 10.2%.

On brands, [Ahrefs' May 2025 study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/) of 75,000 brands and its [December 2025 follow-up](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-brand-visibility-correlations/) found brand mentions correlated far more strongly than backlinks; YouTube mentions were strongest, at about 0.737. SE Ranking, modelling 129,000 domains, says "backlinks remain the strongest signal" for ChatGPT.

### Do citations bring clicks?

Fewer than before. [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) tracked 900 US adults in March 2025. They clicked a result on 8% of visits with an AI summary, 15% without, and a link inside the summary on 1%. [Seer Interactive's 2026 update](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update), across 53 brands, found being cited brought 120% more organic clicks per impression than not being cited, but 38% fewer than informational queries with no AI Overview.

### Figures that do not match their source

- "Wikipedia gets 47.9% of ChatGPT citations." [Profound's page](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns) says 47.9% is Wikipedia's share within ChatGPT's top 10 sources; of all ChatGPT citations it was 7.8%.
- "FAQ sections double ChatGPT citations." SE Ranking's summary says so, but its raw data shows FAQ pages averaging 3.8 citations against 4.1 without.
- "Reddit is ChatGPT's most-cited domain." [Profound's page](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns) puts Wikipedia first at 7.8% of all ChatGPT citations (August 2024 to June 2025) and Reddit at 1.8%. [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/) reports Promptwatch data showing Reddit's share at 3.8% (18 July to 7 August 2026) and a provisional 0.5% average on 14 to 17 August.

## How do you test whether AI recommends you in 15 minutes?

Budget 15 minutes for one round on one tool, then repeat it. [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) had 600 volunteers run 12 prompts 2,961 times. For ChatGPT and Google's AI, the same brand list came back less than 1 time in 100; Claude was only slightly more consistent.

1. Write 20 prompts a buyer would type, in full sentences: "which bookkeeping firms work with seed-stage SaaS startups?", not "bookkeeping SaaS".
2. Paste each into ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI Mode in a fresh chat.
3. Log whether your brand is named, your page is cited, and which competitors appear.
4. Repeat the round on two more days and in the other tools.
5. Your **visibility percentage** is runs that name you, divided by total runs, times 100.

Named in 6 of 60 runs is 10% (our arithmetic). Track that monthly, not single answers.

Two official reports add platform data. Google's [Generative AI performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139?hl=en) reached all sites on 31 August 2026 and shows impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode by page, country, date and device. Bing's [AI Performance report](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/), in public preview since February 2026, counts citations in Copilot and Bing's AI summaries. Neither report names ChatGPT.

## What does not work?

- **llms.txt.** Google's guide says Google Search "ignores" it. [Ahrefs' log study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/) of 137,210 domains found 97% of llms.txt files got zero requests in May 2026. echopulse.media publishes one anyway; it is cheap, not a ranking lever.
- **Keyword stuffing.** The GEO paper found "little to no improvement", and on Perplexity it scored 10% worse than baseline on Position-Adjusted Word Count.
- **FAQ schema as a shortcut.** SE Ranking found pages with FAQ schema averaged 3.6 ChatGPT citations against 4.2 without. Google says AI features need no special schema.
- **Chunking.** Google says there is "no requirement to break your content into tiny pieces." Microsoft leans the other way for Copilot: Bing's February 2026 post says "clear headings, tables, and FAQ sections" help AI systems reference content. Clear structure serves both.
- **Fake or bought reviews.** The FTC's rule at [16 CFR Part 465](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-08-22/html/2024-18519.htm), in force since 21 October 2024, bans fake reviews and paying for reviews of a set sentiment. Google's [structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies) forbid marking up fake reviews.
- **Pages built to game AI answers.** Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) name "attempting to manipulate generative AI responses". Its guide says making separate pages for every query variation or fan-out query, primarily to manipulate rankings or AI responses, violates the scaled content abuse policy.

## FAQ

### Does ChatGPT use Google or Bing results?

OpenAI says ChatGPT search "sometimes partners with other search providers" and links Microsoft's and Shopify's privacy policies as providers' policies, without naming one index. It does require access for OAI-SearchBot.

### Does llms.txt help you get cited?

No platform's documentation asks for it. Google says Search ignores it, and Ahrefs found 97% of the files got no requests in May 2026.

### Can I pay to be cited by ChatGPT?

OpenAI's pages describe no paid route into search citations: eligibility comes from allowing OAI-SearchBot, and placement "is not guaranteed." Ads are separate; OpenAI's OAI-AdsBot only visits pages submitted as ads. Paying for reviews of a set sentiment breaks the FTC rule at 16 CFR 465.4.

### Does Google-Extended stop AI Overviews using my site?

No. Google says Google-Extended "doesn't impact a site's inclusion in Google Search." The Search generative AI control in Search Console decides it, and an exclusion takes effect within 1 to 2 days, though some content may take longer.

### How do I know if Google AI Overviews show my site?

Open Search Console's Generative AI performance report, live for all sites since 31 August 2026. It reports impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode by page.
